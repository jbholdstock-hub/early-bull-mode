How to Trade "Early Bull Mode" (The Weinstein Framework)

1. The Fresh Golden Cross Look for the 50-day MA to cross above the 200-day MA within the last 15 trading days. This signals that short-term buying pressure has officially overpowered the 1-year baseline.
2. Buy Close to the 50 MA Never chase a stock that has already rallied 20% past its 50 MA. Entering within 5% gives you a clear trailing stop-loss directly below the 50 MA line.
3. The Rotation Exit Hold while the price remains above the rising 50 MA. If the stock breaks decisively below the 50 MA or the sector loses momentum, exit and rotate capital to the next leader.
